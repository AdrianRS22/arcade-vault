# NEON DUEL (game jam)

## Game description

> NEON DUEL. Category: VERSUS. Color: cyan. Single-player paddle duel against an adaptive CPU, with spin and rally streaks.

## Pitch

Take the left paddle in a neon duel against a CPU that learns how you play. Hit the ball off the edge of the paddle to aim it, and move as you hit to curve it. Long rallies and win streaks pay more, but the ball gets faster with every hit. Let the CPU score three times and the run is over. The description is a genre-level take with its own name, look and sounds.

## Agent's choices to review first

- Identity: `id = neon-duel`, title `NEON DUEL`, `VERSUS`, `cyan` are provided. `cover-duelo` (already in `globals.css`) and `sort_order = 5` are mine. Cyan is also used by Tetris and Toad Road.
- Match format: no fixed match length; 3 lives, one lost per CPU point, so the score is open-ended.
- Field and physics: 800×600, paddles 14×100, ball bounce angle up to ~55° from the paddle offset, +4% speed per hit up to 900 px/s, physics in 1/120 s sub-steps.
- Spin: from the paddle's velocity at contact (`×0.8`, max 500 px/s²), fading over time. No extra key.
- Controls: `↑` `↓` / `W` `S`, Space to start; no mouse. Touch D-pad up and down.
- Scoring: `100 + 10 × rally` per point (spec 01); in spec 02 `(100 × level + 10 × rally + spin bonus) × streak multiplier (×1 to ×3)`.
- CPU: fixed ramp in spec 01 (300 to 560 px/s); in spec 02 an adaptive `skill` (+0.06 per point won, -0.10 per point lost, level floor).
- Progression (spec 02): level every 5 points, serve speed +20 px/s per level (max 520), +1 life at levels 4, 7, 10 (max 5).
- Sound (spec 02): five synthesized WAV effects generated by a throwaway script, muted by default. Skip them if you prefer a silent game.
- Skins are not included; the `skin-designer` agent adds them after implementation.
