# TOAD ROAD (game jam)

## Game description

> Frogger. "Cruza la carretera y el rio sin convertirte en papilla." The player controls a frog that must cross a road with traffic and then a river (logs/turtles to ride) to reach goal slots at the top, without getting run over or drowning.

## Pitch

Hop a neon toad across four lanes of traffic, then ride logs and turtles over the river to fill all five goal slots. Every cleared round is faster, and the turtles start to dive. The description names a specific commercial game, so this is a genre-level take with its own name (TOAD ROAD), its own lane table and art drawn with canvas shapes.

## Agent's choices to review first

- Identity: `id = toadroad`, title `TOAD ROAD`, `ARCADE`, `cyan`, `cover-rana` (already in `globals.css`), `sort_order = 4`. `ARCADE` is the thickest category; `VERSUS` is multiplayer and `PUZZLE` mislabels the game.
- Field: 16×12 grid of 50 px cells (800×600): goal row, 5 river lanes, median, 4 road lanes, start row. Lane speeds and sizes are in the table of spec 01.
- Rules: 3 lives, 30 s per attempt, hop one cell per key press, bushes and filled slots kill, riding off-screen kills.
- Scoring: 10 per new row, `50 + 10 × seconds left` per slot, `1000` per round clear (`1000 × level` in spec 02).
- Progression (spec 02): +12% speed per level capped at 2.0, timer down to 20 s, diving turtles from level 2, bonus fly (40% per attempt, 200 points), extra life every 10,000 points up to 5.
- Sound (spec 02): five synthesized WAV effects generated by a throwaway script, muted by default. Skip them if you prefer a silent game like Snake.
- Art: everything is drawn with canvas shapes; no assets are imported.
